# Becoming an author

You get listed as an author the moment your first post goes up — there's no separate application. Two ways to do it:

## 1. While writing your first post (easiest)

In the [editor](https://mlsystems.dev/write), pick **Add yourself as author**, fill in your details, and submit. Your profile ships with the post.

## 2. By email

Send your details to **admin@mlsystems.dev** and we'll add you.

---

## What we need

- **Name** — how your byline reads. _(required)_
- **Handle** - your unique username _(required)_
- **Email** — so we can confirm it's really you before your name goes on a post. Used for initial verification only.
- **Bio** — a sentence or two on what you work on. _(optional)_
- **Links** — any of: website, GitHub, X/Twitter, LinkedIn. _(optional)_

That's it. Once you're in, any post can credit you, and you get a profile page and social card automatically.
